FX and Atmosphere

10 patches. Control values are as shown on the plugin panel. Times and frequencies come from measurements of the plugin.

New to this category? Build a patch from init, step by step, hearing each decision.

Mixing this category

Starting points for every patch here. Each patch's own notes cover what's different about it.

  • High-pass at 100-300 Hz, unless the low end is the point, as in Sub Drop and V8 Drone.
  • EQ to taste: automate a high cut rather than setting a fixed one.
  • They rarely need compression. Automate the level instead, and sidechain drones to the kick.
  • Keep low FX centred, and pan or widen high ones for movement.
  • Use reverb throws at the end of a sweep. Mute the source on the downbeat for a clean cut.
