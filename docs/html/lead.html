Mixing this category

Starting points for every patch here. Each patch's own notes cover what's different about it.

  • High-pass at 200-300 Hz.
  • Lift a little around 2-3 kHz to bring it forward, and dip it there if it competes with a vocal.
  • Light compression evens out phrases. It doesn't need sidechain.
  • Centre it, with width coming from its delay and reverb.
  • Give it its own delay send, then duck the send under the dry line if it clouds.

How to use it

Mixing

Mixing

  • VCF KEY FOLLOW to 10The howl tracks your pitch and becomes a more melodic tone.
  • ENV T3 (decay) to 6.0A long, slow howl on every note.

Velocity affects level, so play with dynamics, as a player's breath would vary. Mod wheel deepens the slow wobble: it shares the LFO, so it can't add a faster vibrato.

How to use it

Wistful melodies in slow tracks, memory-like interludes, and intros. Very evocative in a sparse arrangement.

Mixing

  • A gentle high cut around 8 kHz adds to the tape feel.
  • Add tape saturation or a wow-and-flutter plugin if you have one.
  • Plenty of reverb suits it.
  • High-pass at 250 Hz.
  • Built-in reverb: turn it off if you're using your own.

Pairings

  • VCF Cutoff to 7.0A clearer, brighter tone, closer to a piccolo.
