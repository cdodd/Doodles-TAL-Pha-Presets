Mixing this category

Starting points for every patch here. Each patch's own notes cover what's different about it.

  • High-pass at 150-200 Hz, higher in a busy mix.
  • Dip 300-500 Hz if chords cloud.
  • Sidechain to the kick with a slower release than the bass, about 200-300 ms.
  • Let it be the widest part of the mix, and check it in mono.
  • A shared long reverb works well. Keep any delay low, as sustained chords smear it.

Mixing

  • LFO Inv onThe filter jumps open and closes gradually: more of a bloom than a pump.
  • VCF LFO to 6.0A much deeper, almost gated effect.
