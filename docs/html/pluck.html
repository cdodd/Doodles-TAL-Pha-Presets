Mixing this category

Starting points for every patch here. Each patch's own notes cover what's different about it.

  • High-pass at about 200 Hz.
  • Lift 2-4 kHz a little for the attack, or shelve the top down for a softer part.
  • A little compression with a slow attack brings the transient out. It rarely needs sidechain.
  • Pan it off-centre as a counterpart to the lead, or use a stereo delay.
  • Delay suits these more than reverb, as short notes leave room for echoes.
