10 patches. Control values are as shown on the plugin panel. Times and frequencies come from measurements of the plugin.

New to this category? Build a patch from init, step by step, hearing each decision.

Mixing this category

Starting points for every patch here. Each patch's own notes cover what's different about it.

  • High-pass at 100-200 Hz, depending on how much of the left hand you keep.
  • Dip 300-500 Hz if chords get muddy, and lift 2-3 kHz for the tine or bite.
  • Gentle compression evens out chords. Sidechain only in a dense chorus.
  • Keep tight rhythm parts near the centre, and let chorused keys go wide.
  • Use a short plate or room send, and keep it clear of the vocal.

Innocent, nostalgic moments. Lullaby-like melodies and intros.

Mixing
