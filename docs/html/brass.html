New to this category? Build a patch from init, step by step, hearing each decision.

Mixing this category

Starting points for every patch here. Each patch's own notes cover what's different about it.

  • High-pass at about 150 Hz; low sections less.
  • Lift 1-2 kHz for bite, and dip it under vocals.
  • Compress to hold stabs level with each other, with a slow enough attack to keep the front edge.
  • Centre mono parts, and let unison and detuned sections sit wider.
  • A short room or plate send suits stabs. Keep long tails out of fast figures.

Melodic hooks and short solos, or call-and-response with a lead.
