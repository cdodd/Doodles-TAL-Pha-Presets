Mixing this category

Starting points for every patch here. Each patch's own notes cover what's different about it.

  • High-pass around 30 Hz; below that there's little but rumble.
  • Lift 800 Hz to 1.5 kHz a little for small speakers, and leave the lows alone.
  • Sidechain to the kick with a fast attack and about 100 ms release, for 3-6 dB of reduction.
  • Keep it centred. Narrow anything wide below about 120 Hz.
  • Keep reverb and delay off the low end: filter the send, or use none.

Performance

Velocity sets the filter sweep: accent the on-beats for the classic pumping groove. Mod wheel opens the filter for a brighter chorus section.

How to use it

Straight 8ths or 16ths under verse and chorus: the default engine of an outrun track. Program velocity accents rather than playing all notes equal.

Mixing

  • High-pass at 30 Hz; little useful energy sits below the 16' fundamental.
  • Sidechain to the kick with a fast release (around 80-120 ms) so the pump sits with the 8ths.
  • Keep it dead centre; it is already mono.
  • A small 800 Hz to 1.5 kHz lift brings it through on small speakers; don't touch the lows.

Pairings

  • Outrun SixteenthsBass plays 8ths, arp plays 16ths an octave or two up, so the rhythm interlocks without masking.
  • Endless HighwaySlow PWM pad fills the mids above the short bass notes.
  • Gated snare and LinnDrum-style kitThe short decay leaves room for a big snare tail.

Tweak it

  • VCF Cutoff to 4.0Brighter, more aggressive; useful for a final chorus.
  • VCF ENV mode to NormalRemoves velocity response; every note is equally bright and mechanical.
  • ENV T3 (decay) to 4.5Each note stays open longer, turning the rolling feel into a longer growl.
