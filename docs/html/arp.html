Arp

10 patches. Control values are as shown on the plugin panel. Times and frequencies come from measurements of the plugin.

New to this category? Build a patch from init, step by step, hearing each decision.

Mixing this category

Starting points for every patch here. Each patch's own notes cover what's different about it.

  • High-pass at about 250 Hz, or about 120 Hz for bass-register arps.
  • Keep it low in level rather than EQing it. Dip it where the lead sits if they compete.
  • It rarely needs compression. Sidechain lightly to lock it with the bass pump.
  • Keep the notes centred or use a stereo delay; hard-panned arps pull the ear away.
  • Delay sets the rhythm, so sync it to the project and keep feedback low.
