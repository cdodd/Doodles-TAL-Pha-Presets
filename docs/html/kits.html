Starter kits

Five sets of patches that work as a track, each with a tempo, a chord progression and an arrangement to start from. Change anything you like.

Outrun night drive

Outrun · 118 BPM · A minor: Am, F, C, G

The standard recipe, and the best first track: driving and hopeful, with every part in its own register.

Patch Role
Nightdrive Bass: straight 8ths, accents on the beat.
Outrun Sixteenths Arp: held chords, an octave or two above the bass.
Endless Highway Pad: one chord per bar, sidechained.
Sunset Solo Lead: the main melody.
Sunrise Fanfare Brass: stabs on chorus downbeats.

Arrangement

  • Intro: pad and arp, filter closed; open it over eight bars.
  • Verse: add the bass and drums.
  • Chorus: add the lead and brass stabs. Pull the pad down by 2 dB.
  • Breakdown: arp and pad only, then a Riser into the last chorus.

Mix: Sidechain the bass and pad to the kick. The arp stays above about 250 Hz, and the lead sits on top with its own delay.

Darksynth pursuit

Darksynth · 132 BPM · E minor: Em, C, D, Em

Heavy and relentless. Fewer, darker parts leave room for one aggressive lead.

Patch Role
Blacktop Bass: rolling 8ths.
Sequencer Drive Sequence: a tense pattern above the bass.
Night Patrol Brass: long, dark chords.
Cyber Scream Lead: short, aggressive phrases.
Power Fifth Riffs: the heavy chorus hook.

Arrangement

  • Intro: V8 Drone and the sequence, with the filter closed.
  • Verse: add bass and low brass.
  • Chorus: Power Fifth riff with the lead on top. Use Sub Drop on the first downbeat.
  • Breakdown: sequence and Alarm Siren only, for two bars at most.

Mix: High-pass the sequence at about 120 Hz and the riff at about 100 Hz, so that Blacktop keeps the low end. Saturate the bus rather than opening filters.

Dreamwave coastline

Dreamwave · 92 BPM · D major: Dmaj7, Bm7, Gmaj7, A

Slow and wide, with plenty of space. The keys carry the song and everything else floats around them.

Patch Role
Velvet Bass Bass: long, connected notes.
Eighty-Four Tines Keys: the main chord part.
Aurora Haze Pad: slow movement behind the keys.
Cascade Arp: a quiet falling pattern.
Glass Whistle Lead: a sparse, high melody.

Arrangement

  • Intro: keys alone, then the pad fades in.
  • Verse: add bass and soft drums.
  • Chorus: add the arp and the whistle melody.
  • Outro: drop to whistle and pad, with long reverb tails.

Mix: Velvet Bass has low-end stereo from its chorus, so narrow it below 120 Hz. Use light or no sidechain. Long reverbs are fine here, but keep the keys clear.

Chillwave cassette

Chillwave · 84 BPM · F major: Fmaj7, Dm7, Bbmaj7, C

Warm, soft and slightly worn, like a tape you've played for years.

Patch Role
Rubber Coupe Bass: syncopated, with ghost notes.
Warm Tines Keys: gentle jazzy chords.
Ghost Mall Pad: a hazy bed.
Soft Rain Pluck: a quiet broken-chord pattern.
Tape Flute Lead: a wistful melody.

Arrangement

  • Intro: pad and pluck with vinyl noise.
  • Verse: add the bass, keys and a soft drum loop.
  • Chorus: add the flute melody.
  • Outro: remove the drums and let the tape wobble be heard.

Mix: A shared high cut around 6-8 kHz and some tape saturation on the bus tie it all together. Ghost Mall drifts in pitch, so don't tune other parts against it.

Neon pop

Retro pop · 112 BPM · C major: C, Am, F, G

Bright, funky and vocal-friendly. Every part plays a rhythm, so keep each one simple.

Patch Role
Chrome Octave Bass: octave jumps.
Funk Clav Keys: tight rhythm part.
Ensemble Sunset Strings: long chords in the chorus.
Miami Section Brass: syncopated stabs.
Hero Theme Lead: the hook between vocal lines.

Arrangement

  • Intro: Hero Theme hook over bass and drums.
  • Verse: bass and clav under the vocal.
  • Chorus: add strings and brass stabs.
  • Bridge: strings and vocal only, then back in with a brass hit.

Mix: Keep the 1-3 kHz range clear for the vocal: dip the clav and brass there during vocal lines. Automate Hero Theme up only when the vocal isn't singing.
