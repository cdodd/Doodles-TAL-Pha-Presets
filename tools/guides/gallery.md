# Sound gallery

Hear and see what each waveform and key control does. Each sample is an A3 held for two seconds, changing only what's named. Every spectrogram uses the same scale, so you can compare them directly.

## Saw waveforms

The manual shows these as icons. All are the same note, with the filter open.

**Saw.** The full harmonic series, and the usual starting point. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/saw-waveforms-saw.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Saw](img/gallery/saw-waveforms-saw.png)

**Saw 2.** The 2nd harmonic is strongest, so it sounds an octave up and hollow. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/saw-waveforms-saw-2.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Saw 2](img/gallery/saw-waveforms-saw-2.png)

**Saw PW.** Shifts continuously with PWM. Blacktop uses it. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/saw-waveforms-saw-pw.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Saw PW](img/gallery/saw-waveforms-saw-pw.png)

**Saw 3.** Top-heavy and buzzy, with the 8th harmonic strongest. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/saw-waveforms-saw-3.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Saw 3](img/gallery/saw-waveforms-saw-3.png)

**Saw 4.** Strong peaks at the 2nd, 6th and 8th harmonics: bell-like. Glass Chime uses it. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/saw-waveforms-saw-4.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Saw 4](img/gallery/saw-waveforms-saw-4.png)

## Pulse waveforms

Pulse positions can be retuned and synced separately from the saw.

**Square.** Odd harmonics only: hollow. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/pulse-waveforms-square.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Square](img/gallery/pulse-waveforms-square.png)

**Pulse 25%.** Nasal, with every 4th harmonic missing. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/pulse-waveforms-pulse-25.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Pulse 25%](img/gallery/pulse-waveforms-pulse-25.png)

**Pulse PW.** The width moves, so the harmonics do too. This is the classic pad motion. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/pulse-waveforms-pulse-pw.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Pulse PW](img/gallery/pulse-waveforms-pulse-pw.png)

**Saw position.** The plain saw's harmonic series, but it can be retuned and synced separately. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/pulse-waveforms-saw-position.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Saw position](img/gallery/pulse-waveforms-saw-position.png)

## Sub oscillator

Alone at full level, played at A3. Only level 0 turns the sub off.

**Square -1 oct.** The panel labels this position OFF. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/sub-oscillator-square--1-oct.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Square -1 oct](img/gallery/sub-oscillator-square--1-oct.png)

**Pulse 25% -1 oct.** Adds the octave above: more defined. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/sub-oscillator-pulse-25--1-oct.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Pulse 25% -1 oct](img/gallery/sub-oscillator-pulse-25--1-oct.png)

**Mixed -1 oct.** Strong upper partials: the brightest of the -1 octave positions. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/sub-oscillator-mixed--1-oct.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Mixed -1 oct](img/gallery/sub-oscillator-mixed--1-oct.png)

**Bright square -1 oct.** Odd harmonics only, like the square, but with stronger upper ones. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/sub-oscillator-bright-square--1-oct.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Bright square -1 oct](img/gallery/sub-oscillator-bright-square--1-oct.png)

**Square -2 oct.** Very low: for higher notes, rather than bass. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/sub-oscillator-square--2-oct.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Square -2 oct](img/gallery/sub-oscillator-square--2-oct.png)

**Pulse 25% -2 oct.** Adds the octave above the sub, so it reads more clearly than the -2 octave square. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/sub-oscillator-pulse-25--2-oct.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Pulse 25% -2 oct](img/gallery/sub-oscillator-pulse-25--2-oct.png)

## High-pass and bass boost

A saw, played low. The filter has four positions, and 'off' is the second.

**Bass boost.** A broad lift of about 6 dB up to 250 Hz. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/high-pass-and-bass-boost-bass-boost.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Bass boost](img/gallery/high-pass-and-bass-boost-bass-boost.png)

**Bass boost + ALT.** A tighter peak around 80 Hz. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/high-pass-and-bass-boost-bass-boost-and-alt.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Bass boost + ALT](img/gallery/high-pass-and-bass-boost-bass-boost-and-alt.png)

**Off.** Neutral. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/high-pass-and-bass-boost-off.mp3)

![Spectrogram of Off](img/gallery/high-pass-and-bass-boost-off.png)

**HP 1.** A gentle low cut. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/high-pass-and-bass-boost-hp-1.mp3)

![Spectrogram of HP 1](img/gallery/high-pass-and-bass-boost-hp-1.png)

**HP 2.** A strong low cut, down 13 dB at 200 Hz. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/high-pass-and-bass-boost-hp-2.mp3)

![Spectrogram of HP 2](img/gallery/high-pass-and-bass-boost-hp-2.png)

## Envelope and VCA mode

The same filter sweep through the shared envelope, with a different amp mode.

**VCA Normal.** Amp and filter close together: a pluck. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/envelope-and-vca-mode-vca-normal.mp3)

![Spectrogram of VCA Normal](img/gallery/envelope-and-vca-mode-vca-normal.png)

**VCA Gate.** The amp holds and only the filter closes: the note keeps its weight. [Hear it](../audio/gallery/envelope-and-vca-mode-vca-gate.mp3)

![Spectrogram of VCA Gate](img/gallery/envelope-and-vca-mode-vca-gate.png)
